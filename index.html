01About the meeting

Where computation meets Bayesian thinking.

BayesComp is the flagship biennial meeting of the Bayesian Computation Section of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis. The series brings researchers and practitioners together around computational methods for inference and decision making.

The 2027 edition will be hosted at Texas A&M University in College Station. Three keynote speakers are confirmed; additional speakers, sessions, registration, participant support, and venue details will be added as the organizing committees confirm them.
